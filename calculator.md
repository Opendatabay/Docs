## Platform Fees & Commission Schedule

Listing on Opendatabay is free. Opendatabay applies a tiered commission structure on Self-Serve Sales, API calls, and data access fees. The applicable commission decreases progressively as the transaction value increases, providing lower effective rates for higher-value transactions.

The applicable fee is calculated based on the individual transaction value, not on monthly or cumulative sales.

The commission schedule is:

| Sale Price         | Opendatabay Fee (%) | Seller Receives (%) |
| ------------------ | ------------------- | ------------------- |
| $1 - $100          | 30%                 | 70%                 |
| $101 - $1,000      | 30% → 25%           | 70% → 75%           |
| $1,001 - $10,000   | 25% → 20%           | 75% → 80%           |
| $10,001 - $25,000  | 20% → 15%           | 80% → 85%           |
| $25,001 - $50,000  | 15% → 10%           | 85% → 90%           |
| $50,001 - $250,000 | 10% → 5%            | 90% → 95%           |
| $250,001 and above | 5%                  | 95%                 |

For transaction values within a range, the applicable percentage is determined progressively within that range. The exact fee and resulting seller payout are displayed when a Data Provider sets or updates a product price.

After deducting the applicable platform fee, the remaining balance is paid to the Data Provider.
