# Platform Fees and Pricing

## Transaction Fees and Charges

Opendatabay applies a **progressive commission structure** on all **dataset sales, API calls, and data access fees**. The effective commission rate decreases as the transaction value increases, providing lower rates for higher-value transactions.

Opendatabay does **not charge upfront listing fees**. Fees are applied only when a transaction is completed.

The applicable commission is calculated based on the value of each individual transaction and is not calculated cumulatively across multiple transactions.

For transaction values within a range, the applicable commission rate decreases progressively between the stated rates. The exact fee is calculated automatically when the Data Provider sets the sale price.

### Examples

**Example 1:** Selling a dataset for £2,500 → Opendatabay fee is 23.75% (£593.75) and the seller receives £1,906.25.

**Example 2:** Selling a dataset for £25,000 → Opendatabay fee is 15% (£3,750) and the seller receives £21,250.

**Example 3:** Selling a dataset for £50,000 → Opendatabay fee is 10% (£5,000) and the seller receives £45,000.

**Example 4:** Selling a dataset for £100,000 → Opendatabay fee is approximately 8.75% (£8,750) and the seller receives approximately £91,250.

**Example 5:** Selling a dataset for £250,000 → Opendatabay fee is 5% (£12,500) and the seller receives £237,500.

**Example 6:** Selling a dataset for £600,000 → Opendatabay fee is 5% (£30,000) and the seller receives £570,000.

The Opendatabay listing interface displays the applicable fee, fee amount and expected seller payout before a Data Provider publishes or updates a product.

## Payment Terms

### Method of Payment

Payments will be made via **bank transfer**.

### Currency

All payments are made in **GBP (£)**.

### Payout Thresholds

Payments are processed only when total earnings reach a **minimum of £100**.

### Disputes and Refunds

In case of a dispute or refund request, payment may be withheld until the issue is resolved.

### Payment Details

The Data Provider is responsible for providing **accurate payment details**. Opendatabay is not liable for payments sent to incorrect details.

***

## Fee Updates

Opendatabay reserves the right to update commission rates or fees. Data Providers will be notified of any changes **at least 30 days in advance**.

Where a specific Data Product has an agreed fee structure under an existing listing or separate written agreement, that agreed structure will apply to the relevant transaction unless otherwise agreed in writing.

***

## Tax Compliance

The Data Provider is responsible for **declaring and paying any applicable taxes** on earnings from sales through the marketplace, in accordance with local tax regulations.

***

## Anti-Money Laundering (AML) Compliance

The Data Provider must comply with their country's **AML laws and regulations**. Opendatabay may request verification documents to meet legal obligations and may **suspend payments** until the required documentation is provided.
